Chronicle

Your work, your plans and your money, recorded day by day. Private by design.

How your data is handled

Journal

Log what you worked on, its impact and hours, in seconds.

Plan

To-dos and goals with deadlines. Half-fill in a hurry, finish later.

Insights

Charts of your time, categories and goals, ready for appraisal.

Money

Payments recorded from bank SMS, UPI apps and statements, sorted by financial year.

Your data stays yours

Everything lives on your phone. Sign in with Google and Chronicle backs it up to your own Google Drive and keeps a personal Google Sheet there. No Chronicle servers, no ads, no tracking, and nothing to pay.
