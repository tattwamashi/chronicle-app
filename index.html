Chronicle

A personal Android app for keeping your own record: a daily work journal, to-dos and goals, performance insights, and money you spend and receive.

Your data stays yours

Everything is stored on your phone. If you sign in with Google, Chronicle backs it up to your own Google Drive and keeps a personal Google Sheet there. There are no Chronicle servers, no ads and no tracking.

Privacy policy · Terms of use

Chronicle is free and has no paid features.
