Privacy policy

Last updated 29 September 2026

Chronicle is a personal Android app. It has no servers of its own: the people who make it never receive, see or store your data.

What the app stores

What you enter (work journal entries, to-dos, goals, your work profile and pay details) and the payments it records. It keeps all of this on your phone.

Payments from SMS and notifications

If you allow it, Chronicle reads bank SMS and payment-app notifications on your phone to record transactions. It reads these on the phone only. It never uploads the messages anywhere, except as part of your own backup described below.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

If you sign in with Google, Chronicle asks for:

  • Your name and email address, to show which account your data belongs to.
  • A private app folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata), where it saves a backup of your data. Only Chronicle can read this folder. It doesn't give the app access to your other files.
  • Files Chronicle creates (drive.file), such as your personal Google Sheet. The app can't see files it didn't create.

This data goes straight from your phone to your own Google account. Nobody else receives it. Chronicle's use of information from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Sharing

Chronicle doesn't sell or share your data, and it doesn't include ads, analytics or tracking.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app to remove everything on your phone. To remove the backup, open Google Drive settings, then Manage apps, then Chronicle, then Delete hidden app data. Delete your Chronicle sheet from Drive like any other file. You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact
